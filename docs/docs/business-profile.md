# Shoroka Creations Business Profile

## 1. Executive Summary
**Shoroka Creations (Pty) Ltd** (trading as **Shoroka**) is a versatile service-oriented company based in Limpopo, South Africa. As a 100% Black Youth Owned enterprise with a Level 1 B-BBEE status, we are committed to excellence, innovation, and community empowerment. Our philosophy is rooted in building sustainable foundations for future generations.

**Motto:** *"Sons of the soil, seed of the mind!"*
