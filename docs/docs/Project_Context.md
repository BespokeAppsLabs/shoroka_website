# Shoroka Creations Project Context

## Overview
Shoroka Creations (Pty) Ltd is a 100% Black Youth Owned enterprise based in Limpopo, South Africa. The company specializes in Plant Hire, Logistics, Health & Safety, and Construction.

## Business Model
- **Primary Revenue:** Plant hire services (monitored by operators), logistics contracts, and general construction projects.
- **Philosophy:** "Sons of the soil, seed of the mind!" - focusing on sustainable growth and leaving an inheritance for future generations.
- **Target Audience:** Mining sector, construction firms, government infrastructure projects, and community members in Limpopo and Gauteng.

## Strategic Goals
1. Establish Shoroka as a leading investment and empowerment company.
2. Maintain Level 1 B-BBEE excellence.
3. Provide a "First Class experience ALWAYS!" through service quality and operational safety.

## Technical Context
- **Presence:** Website/Landing Page (in progress).
- **Core Operations:** Management of heavy machinery fleet and logistics tracking.

- Always refer to the business profile in `docs/business-profile.md`.
- Prioritize safety and B-BBEE compliance in all recommendations.
