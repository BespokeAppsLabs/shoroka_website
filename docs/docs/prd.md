# PRD: Shoroka Creations Website

## 1. Executive Summary
Provide a professional digital presence for Shoroka Creations to showcase their plant hire, logistics, and construction services.

## 2. Product Scope
- **Landing Page:** Hero section with motto, services overview, about us, and contact info.
- **Service Categories:** Detail pages/sections for Plant Hire, Logistics, Health & Safety, and Construction.
- **Lead Generation:** Clear contact details and potentially a request for quote form.

## 3. User Personas
- **Fleet Managers:** Looking for reliable plant hire and transport.
- **Safety Officers:** sourcing PPE and training.
- **Government Contractors:** verifying B-BBEE status and construction capabilities.

## 4. Success Criteria
- Professional look and feel aligned with "First Class Experience".
- Clear communication of B-BBEE Level 1 status.
- Easy access to service details and contact information.
